## Stats 
Intellect: Increase mana and critical hit with spells
Spirit: Increase health and mana regen.
Stamina: Increas health points
Healing Power: Increase Healing Values (see scaling values)
Casting Speed: Multiplikativ
Spell Hit: Nicht notwendig, da healing
Spell Crit: +50% Heal bei Crit (nur direct heal, nicht HoT)
Spell Haste: Reduziert Cast-Time (nicht GCD, HoTs profitieren nicht von Haste)
Spirit Regen/2s = Spirit/5 + 15 (base mana regen) + MP5-Effects (2s)
Spirit Regen starts 5 seconds after last cast
MP5 Regen starts directly after cast

## Skill Scaling (wir nutzen die für die jeweiligen Skills hinterlegten Faktoren):
Level 20 Threshold: Ranks learned at level 20 or higher get 100% of the intended bonus healing coefficient for that cast time.
Sub-Level 20 Penalty: Ranks learned below level 20 suffer a linear reduction in bonus healing scaling (100% - (20 - level) * 3.75%).
Cast Time Scaling: Bonus healing distribution is heavily tied to the standard 3.5-second rule (shorter cast times scale down bonus healing proportionally)
--> Level Scaling below 20 LS = (100-(20-[SkillLevel])*3.75)/100
--> Cast-Time Scaling CTS = [Skill-CastTime]/3.5
--> Total Low-Level Scaling TLLS = LS * CTS
